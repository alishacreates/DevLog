# Brand Identity
DevLog should feel like a mix of GitHub, Vercel, Linear, and a developer journal.

## Product Name

DevLog

---

## Tagline

Build. Learn. Share.

---

## Brand Mission

Empower student developers to document their journey and grow together through collaboration.

---

## Brand Personality

* Friendly
* Curious
* Professional
* Supportive
* Builder-first

---

## Design Principles

* Clean
* Minimal
* Developer-focused
* Accessible
* Fast

---

## Color Direction

Primary: Electric Blue

Accent: Violet / Purple

Background: Dark Gray / Near Black

Cards: Charcoal Gray

Text: White and Cool Gray

Success: Green

Warning: Amber

Error: Red

---

## Typography

Primary Font

Geist

Code Font

Geist Mono

---

## Voice

Professional but approachable.

Encourage learning rather than competition.

Celebrate progress instead of perfection.
